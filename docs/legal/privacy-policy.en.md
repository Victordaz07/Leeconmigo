# Privacy policy

## 1. Summary

Kitewise is built so that a child’s information stays on the device. It has no accounts, no ads, no analytics or tracking, and it does not sell or share data. It is published by [PENDING].

## 2. What the app stores, only on this device

- The child’s name or nickname as typed by the adult, their avatar, level and goals.
- Practice statistics: reading minutes, stories, questions answered, words that needed help, stars, and how the child said their focus felt.
- Progress in each subject.
- What the child creates or writes in the app: rhythms, paintings, collages and short ideas in “My notebook”.
- The adult’s settings: PIN, reminders, Focus Mode and the acceptance of these texts.

## 3. The child’s voice

The microphone is used only while the child reads aloud inside the app. Audio is turned into text inside the device, with a model that ships with the app, and both live only in temporary memory while the reading lasts.

Voice is never recorded, never saved to files and never leaves the device. Only the statistics in item 2 are kept from a reading.

## 4. What we do not do

- We do not collect personal information on servers of ours: we have no servers for that.
- We do not use ads, analytics, third-party error reporting or tracking tools.
- There are no accounts, chat or contact with strangers.
- The app does not use the camera, location or contacts.
- We do not sell or share anyone’s data.

## 5. Internet

The app works offline and sends no child data to any server. The app store and the device’s system may have their own policies about installing and using apps, which we do not control.

## 6. Reminders

If the adult turns them on, reminders are local notifications scheduled on the device. We use no remote (“push”) delivery and no server to notify. The adult can turn them off in Parent mode or in the phone’s settings.

## 7. Weekly PDF report

The report is created on the device and contains no voice or images. It only leaves the device if the adult chooses to share it with the system share sheet; the adult decides with whom. What happens next depends on the app they choose.

## 8. Backups

Kitewise does not take part in Android’s automatic backups: its data is not uploaded to your Google account. So if you change phones, progress does not transfer.

## 9. Control and deletion

- In Parent mode (with the PIN) you can erase a child’s progress or erase all of the app’s data.
- Uninstalling the app deletes everything stored.
- Because the data is only on this device, we hold no copy to request or delete on servers.

## 10. Children

Kitewise is for elementary-school children (about ages 6 to 12) and is installed and set up by an adult, who accepts these texts on the child’s behalf. Because we do not collect children’s personal information, we hold none of their data.

## 11. Changes

If we change what data the app stores or how it is used, we will update this text and the app will ask the adult to read and accept it again before continuing.
