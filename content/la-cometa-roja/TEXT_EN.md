# The Red Kite

## Page 1

The rain stopped just after lunch. Nia looked across the schoolyard. There was a patch of blue in the sky.

“Now we can try!” she told Teo.

They had waited all week to fly their red kite. Nia took the spool while Teo watched closely. A breeze lifted the kite a little. They both jumped with joy.

## Page 2

Then a strong gust came. The string slipped from Nia’s fingers. The kite rose, spun once, and landed in the big oak. Its blue tail caught on a high branch.

“It’s gone,” Teo whispered.

Nia looked up. They could still see red among the leaves, but the branch was much too high.

## Page 3

Teo stepped toward the trunk.

“We aren’t going to climb,” Nia said. “That branch is too high.”

They sat under the tree for a moment. Teo’s eyes filled with tears. Nia felt sad too, but she saw the school caretaker passing by.

“Let’s ask him for help,” she said.

Nia explained where the blue tail was. He looked in the direction Nia pointed.

## Page 4

The caretaker brought a long pole. Nia and Teo waited several steps from the tree.

The tip touched the kite. The kite moved once. Then again. At last it came free and floated down to the ground.

“It’s back!” Teo cried.

Nia thanked the caretaker. When she picked up the kite, she noticed a small tear near the center.

## Page 5

“It can’t fly like that,” Teo said.

“Not yet,” Nia answered.

At a courtyard table, they laid the kite face up. The caretaker gave them a bit of strong paper and tape. Nia held the kite. Teo smoothed the patch with care.

The patch felt firm. Nia gave the blue tail a very gentle tug to check that it was still tied on.

## Page 6

Later, the breeze returned. Nia took the string, and Teo held the kite until the air lifted it. This time Nia held the spool firmly.

The red kite flew above the oak. Its little patch shone in the sun.

“It looks like a star,” Teo said.

Nia smiled. They had lost the kite for a while, but they found a way to bring it back. Now it flew higher than before.

## Questions

- What made the kite land in the oak?
- Why did Nia ask for help instead of climbing the tree?
- What did Nia and Teo do before flying the kite again?
- What else could you repair instead of throwing it away?
